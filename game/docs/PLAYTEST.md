# Small supervised playtest: protocol and sheet

Status: **not yet run.** No playtest results exist. Do not describe the game as playtested until the table at the bottom has real rows.

## Setup (about 30 minutes per child)
- 3 to 5 children, ages 7 to 12, mix of ages around the age-10 line (two under 10, two 10 and over).
- A parent or teacher present. Get their consent; keep names off the sheet (use P1, P2).
- Device: whatever the child normally uses (phone, tablet, school laptop). Note the device.
- Do not explain the game. Say only: "Try to look after the crew. Tell me what you are thinking."

## Steps
1. The game starts with one short story card, then play. An optional 4-question warm-up is offered at the start of Sol 2 (the game records the before score on the device).
2. Child plays one mission. Observer notes, with timestamps: where they hesitate, what they ask, what they click that does nothing, when they smile or get bored.
3. Science check at the end (after score shown).
4. Ask: "Would you play again? Why or why not?" and "What would you change?" Then, a day or a week later if possible: "Do you remember what a storm shelter is for?"
5. Offer a second play. Whether they take it, and which camp, difficulty and week they pick, answers the replay question.

## What to look for
- Does the age-10 boundary feel abrupt? Did a 9 year old want the scratch camp, or a 10 year old want help? (Pip's help setting can be changed independently.)
- Do children understand the Calm, Watch and Storm strip without reading?
- Do rewards and unlocks make them want another mission?
- Which text did they not read?

## v4 additions to the protocol (no playtest has been run on v4)
Ages 7 to 12, with a parent or teacher present, consent given, no names or recordings kept. Note only: age band, mode played, and the answers below.
- Time from Start to the first meaningful action (planting, or pressing Drive).
- Could the child find and start rover driving without help? Could they stop, turn and get out?
- Did they understand what a marker wanted (rock, ground, photo)?
- Colony tab: could they say what would run out first?
- Pip: ask "What should I do today?" Was the answer useful?
- After play: "What is a Storm day?" and "Is that a NASA warning?" (expected: no, it is the game's level).
- Which mode would they play again?
